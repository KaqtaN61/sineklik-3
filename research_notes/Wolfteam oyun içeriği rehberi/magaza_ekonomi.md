# Wolfteam Mağaza (Dükkan) ve Oyun İçi Ekonomi

> Method note: In this session WebFetch was blocked for every host tried (namu.wiki, joygame.com, uyelik.joygame.com, steamcommunity.com, gamefaqs, wolfory.com, knightpin.com, durmaplay.com, wordpress.com). Every finding below therefore comes from **search-engine result summaries and snippets**, not from reading the full pages. Treat prices and exact numbers as "reported by the cited page". Many sources are 2012-2016 forum or blog posts, so they may be out of date. A re-check is advisable once those hosts are allowed.
>
> Terminology note: the brief mentions "Nar". No source found uses "Nar" as a Wolfteam currency. On the Turkish server the free currency is **Altın** (gold) and the paid one is **Nakit** (Cash). "Nar" is probably a mistake. Do not use it without confirmation.

## 1. What currencies exist and how are they obtained?

### Takeaway
Wolfteam has a two-tier economy plus a third currency. **Altın/Gold** is earned by playing. **Nakit/Cash** is bought with real money; on the Turkish server you top up Joygame's JoyPara and convert it to Nakit. **Kurt Parası (KP) / Wolf Coin (WC)** is hard to earn and comes mostly from boxes and events. **GP (Gelişim Puanı)** is the experience/rank point, not a currency. It is boosted by items and distributed as coupons. Each regional server named the premium currency differently: Aeria used AP, Softnyx Global used Softnyx Cash, and Steam Reboot used Cash plus Wolf Coin login rewards.

### Cited Findings
- On the Turkish server, money is loaded as JoyPara into the Joygame account and converted to Wolfteam Cash/Nakit in the game. Example: 10,000 JoyPara converts to 3,852 Wolfteam Cash. Nakit is spent in the Wolfteam market. — [Durmaplay](https://www.durmaplay.com/en/wolfteam-350-cash); [Durmaplay 45000](https://durmaplay.com/en/wolfteam-45000-cash)
- Resellers sell Nakit packs of 350 / 1,000 / 1,875 / 3,850 / 8,000 / 12,250 / 21,250 / 35,000 / 45,000. One listing is "1,000 JoyPara = 350 Cash". E-pins are for Turkey-region accounts only and are entered on the JoyPara top-up page ("E-Pin" option) or the in-game top-up page. — [Durmaplay](https://www.durmaplay.com/en/wolfteam-350-cash); [Hipopotamya](https://www.hipopotamya.com/en/category/wolfteam)

### Inferences

- Altın ≈ Gold (Aeria/Global/Steam); Kurt Parası (KP) ≈ Wolf Coin (WC); Nakit ≈ Cash/AP/Softnyx Cash. The names change by server but the three-currency structure looks shared.

## 2. What shop categories and item durations exist, with example prices if found?

### Takeaway
The in-game "Dükkan" sells weapons, characters/costumes, wolf (kurt) items, tattoo sets (dövme), packages ("K-Paketi"/sets), and an "Özel" (special) tab for functional items such as name change and clan items. Most items are rented for 1/7/30/90 days. Permanent ("süresiz") items are rare and mostly come from boxes or events. Old fan sources say a section refreshes daily at 11:00.

### Cited Findings
- Older reviews say there are no permanent weapons except the starting guns; rentals are typically 1, 7, 30 or 90 days. — [MMOs.com](https://mmos.com/review/wolf-team); [MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/review/wolf-team)
- A reseller page says Cash can buy weapons for 1-day, 7-day or 30-day rental, or permanently. This conflicts with the reviews above. — [Durmaplay WolfTeam Cash](https://www.durmaplay.com/en/wolfteam-cash)
- A Turkish guide says many weapons and characters are time-limited, so players pay again when an item expires. Official announcements label items such as "30 Günlük". — [Knightpin blog (search snippet)](https://knightpin.com/blog/wolfteam-nakit-fiyatlari)
- A 2012 blog describes a section of items for Nakit and gold that "refreshes every day at 11:00". — [bussinesmanvewolfteam (2012)](https://bussinesmanvewolfteam.wordpress.com/2012/06/30/wolfteam-ana-sayfaenvanter-ve-item-shopdukkan/)
- Steam Reboot: an announcement moved some Cash items to Gold at 5,000 G to 50,000 G, with "no permanent selections". A guide says all items are temporary except default equipment, some items and boxes; card effects are permanent and cannot be turned off. — [Steam Community: Wolfteam Reboot](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2170050); [Steam Guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2974660347)

## 3. How do capsules/boxes/lucky draws work?

### Takeaway
Boxes (kutu: Şans Kutusu, Silah Kutusu, Zafer Kutusu, Dövme Kutusu, KP Kutusu, VIP Kutusu, Özel Eşya Kutusu) are the main gacha. They are bought with Nakit or earned from events. Opening one gives a random reward, and permanent items mostly come only from boxes. Events often sell box bundles with bonus boxes ("1+5", "1+18"). Drop rates are not published in the sources found.

### Cited Findings
- The Şans Kutusu is described as a lottery box that can give some of the weapons and items in the game. An old forum list gives a price of 300 Nakit. — [Turkmmo: WolfTeam İle İlgili Hersey](https://forum.turkmmo.com/konu/532587-wolfteam-ile-ilgili-hersey/)
- On Aeria, some free lucky boxes could be opened only once per day and offered a chance at a special character. — [Turkmmo Aeria](https://forum.turkmmo.com/konu/1666554-wolfteam-aeria-hergun-bedava-sans-kutusu-ozel-karakter-sansi/)
- For a "Legacy/EDS" version (likely a private or other build; uncertain), a guide says opening a box makes the server pick a cryptographic random number among 10,000 steps; the box leaves the inventory and the reward is sent as a gift. The guide covers jackpot rates and a reward ladder. — [Wolfory](https://wolfory.com/blog/wolfteam-item-forge-and-lucky-box-guide?lang=tr)
- Joygame forum: "Süresiz Eşyalar Bu Kutularda!" says permanent weapons could drop from May-only boxes (about 9-10 years old). — [Joygame Forum](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/suresiz-esyalar-bu-kutularda/695cc6d2-a8a8-496e-89c7-c5d44b0148ef)
- Box contents threads: "#1 Wolfteam kutulardan ne çıkar", "Tüm Kutuların İçinden Çıkanlar", "Hangi Kutudan Ne Çıkar". "Kurt Koruması" (wolf protection) can come from VIP boxes. — [Joygame Forum](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/1-wolfteam-kutulardan-ne-cikar/cf737aaf-fc2d-4c44-b635-8d0c69af0ec6); [Turkmmo](https://forum.turkmmo.com/konu/2462942-wolfteam-tum-kutularin-icinden-cikanlar-resimli-tanitim/); [Turkmmo](https://forum.turkmmo.com/konu/3582991-wolfteam-hangi-kutudan-ne-cikar/); [Dijipin](https://www.dijipin.com/haber/wolfteam/wolfteam-kurt-korumasi-hangi-kutudan-cikar)

## 4. What premium/VIP or subscription benefits exist?

### Takeaway
The Turkish server has a spending-based **VIP system**. Cash spending earns coupons or tickets that are redeemed in a VIP shop; more spending unlocks better items. Monthly "Avantajlar" pages list VIP items, such as 60-day tattoo sets, 1M GP coupons and 60-day weapons. There is also "Wolfteam Plus Kafe", an internet-café program. No classic monthly subscription was found.

### Cited Findings
- A 2013 Joygame forum post says players who spend Nakit get coupons for VIP shop items, and more spending gives better items. — [Joygame Forum: [D] Wolfteam VIP Sistemi](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/d-wolfteam-vip-sistemi/8ab571a0-3d4d-11e3-b73c-e0db5515edbd)
- Nisan Avantajları (April): VIP shop items include "60 Günlük Asalet Dövme Seti (SP)", "30 Adet 1 Milyon Gelişim Puanı Kuponu" and "60 Günlük EM-212 Yeşil". Tatil Avantajları includes a "30 Günlük Mavi Kurt Dövme Seti". — [Nisan Avantajları](https://uyelik.joygame.com/etkinlik/Wolfteam/nisan-avantajlari-4223887); [Tatil Avantajları](https://uyelik.joygame.com/etkinlik/Wolfteam/tatil-avantajlari-4224914)
- "Wolfteam Plus Kafe" page on joygame.com (internet café benefits; contents not readable). — [Joygame Wolfteam Plus Kafe](https://www.joygame.com/wolfteam/topluluk/wolfteam-plus-kafe)

## 5. How does the inventory and equipping work (slots, presets)? Plus trading, refund and upgrade

### Takeaway
The inventory (Envanter) holds owned items and is where weapon groups (sets) are built for use in matches. Characters and wolves have separate gear: tattoos and wolf items for the wolf form. Items expire when their rental ends. Gifting from the shop is possible (Hediye Et with the recipient's nickname). Direct player-to-player trading is not supported officially. Nakit refunds are generally not given. No durability or repair system and no stat-upgrade forge were found for the official server.

### Cited Findings
- On Aeria (2010), the inventory shows owned items and is where weapon groups are set up for use in game. The Game Shop is separate. — [wolfteams.wordpress](https://wolfteams.wordpress.com/2010/08/13/getting-started/)
- A 2012 Turkish blog covers "Ana Sayfa, Envanter ve Item Shop (Dükkan)". — [bussinesmanvewolfteam](https://bussinesmanvewolfteam.wordpress.com/2012/06/30/wolfteam-ana-sayfaenvanter-ve-item-shopdukkan/)
- Softnyx official game guide (Interface page) exists. — [Softnyx Game Guide](http://wolfteam.softnyx-is.com/GameInfo/Guide/Interface.aspx)
- Gifting: pick a product in the market and choose "Hediye Et", then enter the friend's nickname. A seller states one unit per gift and a daily gift limit of 2,500₺ (seller rule). — [GecBunlari](https://gecbunlari.com/wolfteam-nakit-yukleme-nasil-yapilir/); [Joygame Forum: Nasıl hediye gönderilir](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/nasil-hediye-gonderilir/65830f16-bd9c-41d2-8ae7-f20ee4dcf2a9); [itemsatis](https://www.itemsatis.com/urunler/wolfteam/wolfteam-21250-nakit-50000-joypara/hediye-gonder.html)
- Joygame gift coupons can be bought and the code shared. — [GecBunlari](https://gecbunlari.com/wolfteam-nakit-yukleme-nasil-yapilir/)
- Refunds conflict. One guide says unused Nakit can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Resellers say no refunds. — [GecBunlari](https://gecbunlari.com/wolfteam-nakit-yukleme-nasil-yapilir/); [Playsultan](https://www.playsultan.com/wolfteam-nakit-joypara?dil=en); [Kabasakal](https://kabasakalonline.com/en/wolfteam/nakit)
- Steam Reboot: card effects are permanent and cannot be turned off. There was a buggy shop where prices updated only after re-entering, and the "Charge" button sometimes did nothing. — [Steam Guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2974660347); [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2170050/discussions/0/)
- "Item Forge/EDS" appears in a Legacy/EDS guide. It may not apply to the official server. — [Wolfory](https://wolfory.com/blog/wolfteam-item-forge-and-lucky-box-guide?lang=tr)

### Inferences
- Since rentals are the main model, the expiry date stands in for durability. No repair system was found.

- Number of weapon slots per set (primary/secondary/melee/throwable) and the number of presets were not confirmed.
- Sell-back of items for gold was not found.
- Server history beyond the regional dates (Aeria NA/EU 2009/2010, Germany 2010) is not covered; the 2006 Korean origin and timeline need another source.
