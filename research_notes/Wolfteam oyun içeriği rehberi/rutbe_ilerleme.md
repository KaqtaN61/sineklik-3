# Wolfteam Rütbe (Rank) ve İlerleme Sistemi

> **Method note:** WebFetch was blocked in this session (DNS/allowlist failures for fandom.com, breezewiki, technotoday.com.tr, wordpress.com). Every finding below comes from **search-engine result snippets and summaries**, not full-page reads. Exact numbers should be checked against the full pages before publication. Sources disagree in many places because of server and era differences (Softnyx global/Latin, Aeria Games NA/EU, Joygame TR, and private servers).

## 1. What is the full ordered list of ranks, and roughly how many are there?

### Takeaway
Wolfteam uses a military-style ladder. It starts at Recruit / Acemi Er (0 EXP/GP) and is split into large classes (Enlisted → NCO/Sergeant → Officers → Generals → special top tiers). Most rank names have sub-classes such as Junior/Ast, normal, Senior/Kademeli, and Top/Üst. The Aeria wiki gives **62 ranks**. A Turkish 2025 forum list counts **70+ ranks in 7 main categories**. The top of the ladder differs by server: Martial General and Union General on Softnyx, and Mareşal / Paşa / Kahraman / Tümen Başı / Baş Komutan in Turkish lists.

### Cited Findings
**General structure / count**
- In WolfTeam, ranks are grades a player advances through once enough experience points are gained. "There are a total of 62 ranks available in WolfTeam." — [WolfTeam Aeria Wiki – Ranks](https://wolfteamaeria.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks) (mirror: [breezewiki](https://breezewiki.discard.no/wolfteamaeria/wiki/Ranks))
- The Enlisted class has five ranks (Recruit, Private, Private First Class, Corporal, Sergeant), each split into junior / standard / senior / top sub-classes. — [WolfTeam Info Wiki – Rankings](https://wolfteaminfo.fandom.com/wiki/Rankings)
- A 2025 Turkish list says there are 70+ ranks in 7 main categories. It splits them into "Gönüllü Askerler" (volunteer soldiers) and "Bölüm Subayları" (section officers). — [Serverlar.com.tr – Wolfteam Rütbeler 2025](https://www.serverlar.com.tr/konu/wolfteam-rutbeler-2025-guncel-rutbe-sistemi-ve-gelisim-puanlari-tam-liste.1652/)
- Turkish sources give **Acemi Er** as the lowest rank and **Baş Komutan** as the highest. — [Technotoday](https://technotoday.com.tr/wolfteam-rutbeleri/); [YaCevap](https://yandex.com.tr/yacevap/c/oyun/q/wolfteam-de-kac-seviye-var-205883636). Another YaCevap answer names "General" as the highest, and pvpcim names "Wolfteam Komutanı". — [YaCevap](https://yandex.com.tr/yacevap/c/oyun/q/wolfteamda-kac-tane-rutbe-var-3139567755); [pvpcim](https://pvpcim.com/threads/wolfteam-rutbe-isimleri-ve-rutbe-atlama-tablosu.1298/)

### Inferences
- Name mapping: Martial General on Softnyx/global ≈ the Mareşal/Paşa class in Turkish lists, and Union General ≈ the Tümen Başı / Baş Komutan class (inference from names and threshold sizes, unconfirmed).
- The 62 count (Aeria) and the 70+ count (TR 2025) do not conflict. The TR server appears to have added extra top tiers (Paşa, Kahraman, and so on) over time.
- The top ranks are relative (based on leaderboard position or percentage), so a player can drop rank even with the same GP. This follows from the percentage and quota system, but no direct source confirms it.

### Gaps
- The exact Sergeant-class names and GP values between 47,500 and 271,000, and between 271,000 and 865,000 (Kıdemli Çavuş, Üstçavuş, Astsubay, and so on), were not confirmed from full pages.
- Rank icon descriptions (stripes, stars, colors) are not in the snippets. The only confirmed point is that each rank has a "Mark" icon.
- No source was found on GM/staff ranks (a special GM icon). There is also no information from namu.wiki or Korean sources.
- There is no dated changelog showing when the rank system changed (Martial/Union additions, TR's Paşa/Kahraman tiers).

## 2. How much XP is needed per rank, and how is XP earned?

### Takeaway
In Wolfteam, "EXP" and "GP" are effectively the same accumulated point total for rank. GP is both rank experience and a spendable in-game currency; Altın (Gold) and Nakit/Cash are separate currencies. GP comes from match performance (kills, rounds, mode, playtime). It is boosted by % bonus items or characters (+30%, +100%, +200%), "Yanan Bonus" events, and paid "Harcakazan" events.

### Cited Findings
- The wolfteam-liga table uses the "EXP/GP" column for the threshold, and Turkish sources say "you need GP to rank up". — [wolfteam-liga.de](http://www.wolfteam-liga.de/ranks); [YaCevap](https://yandex.com.tr/yacevap/c/oyun/q/wolfteam-1-rutbe-kac-puan-2914229298)
- During the "Yanan Bonus Etkinliği" (bonus event), GP and gold come in faster. Modes such as Uyku Modu, S2 Yıkımı and Kurt Avı are said to give high experience. — [YaCevap](https://yandex.com.tr/yacevap/c/oyun/q/wolfteam-1-rutbe-kac-puan-2914229298)
- An old forum tip: some Nakit (cash) characters give **+30% GP**. — [Turkmmo – GP/XP kasma](https://forum.turkmmo.com/konu/2979178-wolfteam-gp-ve-xp-kasma-taktikleri/); [Turkmmo – Kd,gp,altın,kp kasma](https://forum.turkmmo.com/konu/3584874-wolfteam-kd-gp-altin-kp-kasma/)
- Old forum: in a 9-round S2 match, taking at least two kills per round can give **150–200 GP**. In paid-resource Ex-İşgal matches, 20–30 minutes on a weekend with +100% GP can give **3–4K GP**. — [bussinesmanvewolfteam (2012)](https://bussinesmanvewolfteam.wordpress.com/2012/06/30/wolfteam-hizli-rutbe-atlama-ve-taktikler/); [Turkmmo](https://forum.turkmmo.com/konu/3584874-wolfteam-kd-gp-altin-kp-kasma/)
- 100% GP and Altın bonuses speed up ranking. Completing daily missions, joining events and using XP/GP booster items are named as the most effective methods. — [GamerMarkt](https://www.gamermarkt.com/tr/urunler/wolfteam-nakit)
- In the Joygame September 2018 "Görev Sistemi" event, reaching 50 points earned **"200% extra GP in all modes"**. — [Joygame Forum – 21-27 Eylül Görev Sistemi Etkinlikleri](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/21--27-eylul-gorev-sistemi-etkinlikleri/8afeda9c-325f-4e80-a66a-0889a3f53e22)
- Spending Nakit during "Harcakazan" events sends extra GP, gold or crates to the gift box. Nakit is a premium balance bought only through e-pins. — [Playsel](https://playsel.com/tr-TR/wolfteam)
- In-match currency: SP points rise with kills, and you choose one of four equipment slots based on SP. Only the first equipment slot is available in the first round. (This is a match-internal resource, separate from rank GP.) — [WolfTeam Türkiye Blog – Başlangıç Rehberi](https://wolfteamtrblog.blogspot.com/p/wolfteam-baslangc-rehberi.html)
- Legacy (a private/remake server): eligible completed PvP matches pay Gold and GP by daily reward tiers, and training matches give no GP. — [WolfTeam Legacy (Wolfory) blog](https://wolfteamlegacy.com.tr/blog/wolf-transformation-guide?lang=tr)
- Kurt Parası (KP) is a separate currency. The "Taç Sistemi" event charged an Altın entry fee and rewarded KP, for example 750,000 Altın entry → 1,000,000 KP after 1,000 item throws. — [Joygame Forum – Eylül'e Özel Taç Sistemi](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/eylule-ozel-tac-sistemi-oyunda/85fd3169-adcd-4971-87bf-87cd35b92a6b); [Turkmmo – KP nedir](https://forum.turkmmo.com/konu/795247-wolfteamda-kp-nedir/)
- Unofficial new-member gift claim: 100,000 Altın, 300,000 KP, 15,000 Nakit and a 7-day inventory. This is from an unofficial-looking site and unconfirmed. — [Prime Wolfteam](https://wolfteam.wtfix.com/)

### Inferences
- Getting from Recruit to Colonel takes about 10–13M GP. At the old forum rate of about 150–200 GP per match without bonuses, that is thousands of matches. This explains why bonus items and events are so central to progression (inference).
- Because GP is both the rank point total and a currency, it is not clear whether spending GP lowers rank. Since rank thresholds are based on "accumulated" GP, spending probably does not lower rank, but this is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- No official formula for GP per kill, win or minute was found.
- No reliable source was found on the WT+ (premium) system's GP/Altın bonus percentages. A Joygame thread titled "WT+ Sisteminin Ayrıcalıkları" exists but could not be read: [link](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/wt-sisteminin-ayricaliklari/9f3bddae-cf9e-434a-8ed5-0da8ada21aeb)
- No direct source was found on whether spending GP reduces rank.

## 3. What mission/quest/achievement/daily reward systems exist?

### Takeaway
Joygame TR has used a step-based **daily "Görev Sistemi" (mission system) event**, attendance-style **login rewards** (time-limited events), the "Taç Sistemi" mission event, and anniversary and seasonal events. No reliable source was found describing a permanent achievement, title or medal system.

### Cited Findings
- Daily 3-step format from September 2018: (1) log in → Power Box; (2) reach 50 points → +200% GP in all modes; (3) play 10 rounds → 1-day human SG back tattoo. Rewards change daily, e.g. on 23 September: "win 10 times on the blue team in Death Game → 1-day 1911 Elite" and "20 wolf kills in Death Game → 1-day Flame Claw". — [Joygame Forum – Görev Sistemi Etkinlikleri](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/21--27-eylul-gorev-sistemi-etkinlikleri/8afeda9c-325f-4e80-a66a-0889a3f53e22)
- "Taç Sistemi": paid-entry tasks. You pay Altın to enter and get KP as the reward. — [Joygame Forum – Taç Sistemi](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/eylule-ozel-tac-sistemi-oyunda/85fd3169-adcd-4971-87bf-87cd35b92a6b)
- June event: a reward for each day you log in. If you miss a day, you continue from the reward you had reached. Rewards are not given automatically and must be claimed. — [Joygame – Wolfteam Haziran Ayı Etkinlikleri](https://uyelik.joygame.com/etkinlik/wolfteam/haziran-etkinlik-3841569)
- Other event pages are built around in-game steps such as playing a set number of rounds or reaching a score. — [Joygame – Yaz Şenlikleri](https://uyelik.joygame.com/etkinlik/Wolfteam/yaz-senlikleri-4237490); [Yaz Tatili Fırsatları](https://uyelik.joygame.com/etkinlik/Wolfteam/yaz-tatili-firsatlari-4237732); [Wolfteam 15. Yıl](https://uyelik.joygame.com/etkinlik/Wolfteam/wolfteam-15yil-4221291)
- About 14 years ago, a user on the Joygame forum suggested daily missions with Nakit/Altın rewards. This suggests there was no permanent daily mission system at the time. — [Joygame Forum – Günlük Görevler Olsun](https://www.joygame.com/forum/wolfteam/konu/gunluk-gorevler-olsun-nakit-ve-altin-hediyesi/8aacfa05-3d4d-11e3-b73c-e0db5515edbd)
- A third-party guide says WC (cash) can be earned through story tasks, events, daily logins and referrals. — [DostGame](https://www.dostgame.com/en/blog/wolfteam-nakit-nasil-kazanilir)
- Clans: a weekly clan ranking and clan wars (WolfTeam Diriliş, a private/remake server). — [wolf2team.com](https://wolf2team.com/klan-siralamasi/)

### Inferences
- In the TR version, missions and attendance appear mostly as **time-limited events** rather than a permanent menu. Event dates (2013 suggestion → 2018 Görev Sistemi event) suggest mission mechanics became established later in the game's life (inference).

### Gaps
- No source was found on a permanent achievement, medal or title system, or on rank-up rewards (items given on ranking up).
- No information was found on weekly mission structure, a battle pass, or a season system on the official servers.
- No Korean original (namu.wiki) or Softnyx global mission details were found.

## 4. Are there rank-restricted rooms/channels or rank-gated items?

### Takeaway
No reliable source was found that confirms rank-restricted channels or rank-gated items. The only confirmed rank-related restriction is the **number quota on the top general ranks** (32/16/8/4/1) and the percentage-based top tiers.

### Cited Findings
- General ranks are limited by player count: Brigadier General 32, Major General 16, Lieutenant General 8, General 4, General of the Army 1. — [WolfTeam Info Wiki](https://wolfteaminfo.fandom.com/wiki/Rankings)
- The TR top tiers (Mareşal and others) are set by percentage of players with 100M/500M GP. — [Serverlar 2025](https://www.serverlar.com.tr/konu/wolfteam-rutbeler-2025-guncel-rutbe-sistemi-ve-gelisim-puanlari-tam-liste.1652/)
- Searches for "acemi kanalı rütbe sınırı" (beginner channel rank limit) did not return a relevant result. — (search: no result)

### Inferences
- A beginner channel or rank range, common in similar games, may exist, but this research could not confirm it.

### Gaps
- Rank requirements for channels or servers (beginner, Free, Clan channels) are unconfirmed.
- No information was found on rank-gated items in the store or rank-based weapon unlocks.
